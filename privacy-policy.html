  • Account Information: Name, email address, password

When you use VividMed, we automatically collect:

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.2 Communication

  • Send you educational content and study reminders

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

5.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your account information at any time through your account settings.

You can opt out of promotional emails by:

5.3 Account Deletion

You may delete your account by contacting us at support@vividmed.com. Upon deletion:

You can request a copy of your data in a portable format by contacting support@vividmed.com.

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:
